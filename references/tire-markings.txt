Tire Vision AI reference notes. Project-authored educational material, not a manufacturer specification or a reproduced standards document. Verify the tire and vehicle documentation for actual fitment and ratings.

Tire size notation: In a marking such as 205/55 R16, 205 represents nominal section width in millimetres, 55 is the sidewall height as a percentage of that width, R indicates radial construction, and 16 is the nominal wheel rim diameter in inches. D denotes diagonal construction; B denotes belted construction. This describes a marking; it does not determine vehicle compatibility. Reference for further reading: NHTSA TireWise, https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/tires .

Load index and speed rating: The service description can follow the tire size, as in 205/55 R16 91V. 91 is a load index, a lookup code rather than kilograms; V is a speed category rather than a recommended driving speed. This application does not include a complete certified rating table. It must not invent a load capacity or maximum speed from an absent reference. Refer to the tire manufacturer's published rating chart and the vehicle placard. Reference for further reading: NHTSA TireWise, https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/tires .

DOT and manufacturing date: On modern tires the last four digits of a complete DOT Tire Identification Number encode the manufacturing week and year. For example, a terminal 2323 identifies week 23 of 2023. A standalone four-digit number is insufficient evidence for a manufacturing date. A partial DOT marking may omit the date, and the full marking may be on the other sidewall. Three-digit historical date codes are not interpreted by this application. Source for further reading: NHTSA TireWise, https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/tires .

Marking terminology: TUBELESS describes a tire intended for use without an inner tube. M+S is a mud-and-snow marking; additional winter qualification markings have separate requirements. XL or EXTRA LOAD is a marking to consult alongside the manufacturer's specifications. Do not infer roadworthiness, tire damage, wear, pressure, legal compliance or overall safety from OCR sidewall text. Brand text recognition is not authentication. OCR confidence represents the recognizer's score and has not been calibrated for tire markings.
